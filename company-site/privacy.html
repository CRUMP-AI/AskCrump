Privacy Policy

The short version: your data stays yours.

Effective: October 13, 2026

Clever Crump ("we," "us") makes software products including Ask Crump, Dabblebit, and Rally. This policy covers this website and how we handle information across our company. Each product also has its own privacy policy with product-specific details — where they differ, the product policy applies to that product.

What we collect on this site

Almost nothing. This website is a static site: it doesn't run accounts, doesn't set tracking cookies, and doesn't fingerprint you. If you email us, we keep your message long enough to reply — that's it.

What our products collect

Our products need some information to work — accounts, saved progress, purchases. The rule across everything we make:

  • We collect the minimum needed for the product to do its job.
  • We never sell your personal information. Not to advertisers, not to data brokers, not to anyone.
  • We don't use your data to train models for other people or share it with third parties for their own purposes.

For specifics, see the privacy policy for the product you use: Ask Crump, Dabblebit.

What we never do

  • Sell or rent your personal information.
  • Share your data with advertisers or data brokers.
  • Track you across other companies' apps and websites.

Data security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information — encrypted connections, access controls, and reputable infrastructure providers. No system is perfect, but we treat your data like we'd want ours treated.

Children

Our products are not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in a meaningful way, we'll note the new effective date above. Continued use of our sites or products after a change means you accept the updated policy.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email support@clevercrump.com and we'll answer plainly.
