# “Work That Continues” source prompt set

These are the normalized production prompts used to create the six storyboard anchors with the
built-in image-generation path. Frames 2–5 used the preceding generated images as continuity
references. Frame 6 used the full sequence as a palette and campaign-style reference.

## Shared identity and visual lock for frames 1–5

Photorealistic vertical 9:16 premium technology-campaign photography. Preserve one poised Black
woman in her late 30s with warm brown skin, shoulder-length natural curls, a tailored charcoal blazer
over a soft ivory top, and small gold stud earrings. Keep her face, age, skin tone, hair, wardrobe,
body proportions, and natural anatomy consistent. Use a restrained charcoal, black, walnut, ivory,
and muted-gold palette with realistic materials, editorial texture, and calm global AI-company
polish. No brand marks, logos, readable text, captions, watermarks, neon effects, holograms,
generated-looking typography, or distorted hands, faces, devices, paper, or furniture.

## Frame 1 — Fragmented work

Establish the protagonist before sunrise in a modern but lived-in home office with charcoal and warm
walnut surfaces. Use a cinematic three-quarter side view as she focuses naturally on a slim dark
laptop. Place a few neatly scattered research printouts, one closed notebook, a phone, and a dark
cup on the desk. Balance cool blue dawn through a large window with one warm practical lamp. Leave
breathing room above. Avoid staged frustration, luxury cliché, or clutter.

## Frame 2 — One workspace

Continue the exact protagonist, room, desk, devices, and dawn lighting. Use an over-the-shoulder
medium close-up from slightly behind her right side. Her hands rest naturally near the trackpad and
one printed brief. Make the laptop the visual focus and show a practical dark-mode productivity
workspace with one conversation column, a compact project/file rail, and two subtle artifact preview
tiles. Use text-free interface blocks only; keep all UI inside the screen.

## Frame 3 — Finished outputs

Continue the exact identity, wardrobe, room, desk, and laptop from a clean three-quarter angle on the
opposite side. Align the former loose research into one tidy stack. On the laptop, show three
polished, text-free artifact previews in a calm grid: one presentation slide, one document page, and
one small research summary card. Give the protagonist a subtly relieved, focused expression. Warm
the window slightly as sunrise develops. Avoid magic, particles, celebration, or dense concept UI.

## Frame 4 — Work continues

Continue the same morning and identity. Show a vertical medium-full shot as the protagonist rises
from the same desk, lowers the closed slim laptop toward a minimalist black work tote, and holds a
phone displaying the same calm, text-free dark workspace. Turn her three-quarters toward the doorway
with a composed, purposeful expression. Keep the room recognizable and the phone, fingers, bag
straps, laptop, and body geometry natural.

## Frame 5 — Work delivered

Continue with the exact protagonist later that morning in a restrained modern meeting room with
charcoal walls, a warm wood table, and soft daylight. She calmly presents to two seated colleagues
seen mostly from behind and softly out of focus. A wall display shows one elegant, minimal,
text-free presentation slide with image and chart blocks; an open laptop on the table carries the
same slide. Use a natural presenting gesture and small remote. Avoid keynote glamour, applause,
giant charts, or stock-photo expressions.

## Frame 6 — End card

Create a new abstract vertical 9:16 end card using the campaign’s restrained visual language. Use a
deep near-black matte charcoal studio space. Place three barely raised dark rectangular planes near
the lower edge, suggesting conversation, project context, and finished work. Connect them with one
thin warm-gold light path that continues toward a distant horizon. Leave a large clean central area
completely empty for the official logo and tagline added in post. No text, letters, logos, icons,
people, devices, recognizable brand shapes, particles, portals, lens flares, or excessive gold.
