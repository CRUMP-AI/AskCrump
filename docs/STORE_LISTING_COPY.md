### Description

The desktop workspace keeps Today, Tasks, Chats, Projects, Library, Studios, and You in one clear rail. Today puts what needs your attention first — approvals waiting on you, work in progress, and finished results — above the Ask Crump composer. Tasks keeps a clear record of agent work and approvals. Chats opens private conversation history. Projects keep instructions, reference context, conversations, files, and creations together. Library is a calm shelf for saved long-form work. Studios brings the released Image and Video tools together. You contains settings, sessions, purchase restoration, privacy, and account controls. On mobile, all seven destinations stay in the bottom bar.
