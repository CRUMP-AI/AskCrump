# Ask Crump candidate-video creative QA

Date: 2026-08-29

Scope: five owner-supplied MOV files reviewed locally; no file was uploaded or published

## Decision

Do not publish any of the five candidates in its current form. Several individual shots are
attractive, but the finished clips do not yet communicate the product outcome or meet the restrained
brand standard required for an acquisition ad.

| Candidate | Technical read | What works | Release blocker | Disposition |

| video 2 (1).MOV | 35.99 s, 960 × 960 | Glass and storm-at-sea shots have usable atmosphere; clean branded bookends | A letterboxed montage with no product narrative, no user outcome, and no focused call to action | Preserve as concept exploration only |

Audio ownership, provider provenance, likeness rights, and commercial-use terms were not established
by the local visual review. They remain mandatory release checks even for a visually approved edit.

## Reusable material

- The horse-on-water opening from video 4.MOV is the strongest standalone motion study.
- The bird close-up from video (3).MOV shows convincing detail and controlled depth.
- The glass macro from video 2 (1).MOV is the most restrained object shot.
- The existing generated logo sequences should not be reused. Composite the official master assets
  in post to prevent letterform and mark distortion.

None of those moments should be presented as current-product proof without clear provenance and a
truthful description of the workflow that produced it.

## Replacement direction

Use the six-frame “Work That Continues” storyboard in
docs/ASK_CRUMP_AD_STORYBOARD_2026-08-29.md. It replaces spectacle with a simple problem →
workspace → artifact → continuity → outcome sequence, holds one protagonist and palette, and reserves
all brand typography for post-production.

## Creative acceptance checklist

- One audience and one problem are clear by the first three seconds.
- The product behavior is recognizable without requiring readable generated text.
- Every shot advances the same story; no unrelated visual montage remains.
- Identity, hands, devices, screen perspective, and room geometry are stable frame to frame.
- The official logo is composited only in post and remains untouched.
- Music, voice, source images, model output, and likenesses have documented commercial rights.
- The final edit uses one CTA and links to a landing page with matching attribution.
- A sound-off review and a reduced-size mobile review both remain understandable.
