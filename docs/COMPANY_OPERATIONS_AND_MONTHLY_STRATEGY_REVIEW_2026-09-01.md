## Product delivery and feedback

The latest product work directly addresses observed user friction rather than adding another broad
capability:

- conversation scrolling is fully user-controlled;
- a restrained offscreen-response cue replaces forced movement;
- creation sheets contain focus and restore it correctly;
- Files search, filtering, sorting, preview layering, and downloads are repaired;
- Video is a visible primary destination and keeps generation work durable while the user explores;
- image references are normalized before provider use and rejection recovery is specific;
- Precision Edit now supports manual Brush/Erase selection and deterministic restoration of every
  protected pixel outside the selected region; and
- no person, race, or ethnicity detector or change-race control was added.

The strongest qualitative feedback theme is now **control**: do not move the user's viewport, do not
hide work behind another layer, do not make a user rediscover a background job, and do not change
parts of an image they did not select. Natty's image/video testing exposed real failures in
reference-image acceptance, safety-rejection recovery, identity/appearance drift, logo fidelity,
and background generation continuity. Those observations are valuable defect discovery, not a
customer-satisfaction sample.

Support evidence is limited. The dedicated public inbox recheck at 09:22 EDT still showed only three
known setup/test threads and no external support, creator, partner, press, bounce, warning,
unsubscribe, abuse, or customer-content message. The absence of tickets at this scale is not proof
that the experience is self-explanatory.

## Product-market-fit and monetization assessment

Product-market fit is **not demonstrated**. Historical usage proves that two people could receive
useful AI output, while the current evidence does not yet prove that an external user keeps work,
returns, recommends the product, or pays. The highest-leverage wedge remains continuing work:
Projects, editable files, and the ability to resume from another session or device.

Monetization readiness is stronger than monetization evidence. The public catalog and checkout
routes exist, but there is no external payer, recognized revenue, refund, or unit-cost baseline.
Do not change prices, discounts, quotas, or Enterprise positioning this month. The current
`Enterprise` tier is a high-capacity individual tier; do not imply SSO, teams, administration,
procurement, SLA, or organization controls.
