## Required measurement boundary

Prefer a new aggregate-only service-role RPC rather than changing the existing weekly export's
return type. A private natural-attempt fact may contain only the owning account, production
environment, fixed `PROJECT_LIMIT_REACHED` reason, UTC attempt time, and deterministic idempotency
key. Public, anonymous, and authenticated execution must be denied.

The backend may record only after the Projects route proves a natural limit denial and before the
dormant experiment claim path. Recorder failure must be fail-open: the existing denial status and
copy remain unchanged. No assignment, exposure, plan opening, checkout, prompt, response, Project
name, filename, URL, or synthetic backfill belongs in this fact.

The aggregate must separately report:

- accounts with at least one active nonempty Project;
- accounts with at least two distinct active nonempty Projects;
- natural limit-attempt accounts;
- natural limit-attempt accounts that already had two qualifying Projects;
- post-attempt recovery-origin `PlanCenterViewed`; and
- explicit denominators with null/unavailable rates when the denominator is zero.

A nonempty assistant response or a ready, nonzero, nondeleted file may qualify a Project. Bare,
archived, deleted, internal, unverified, preview, and development records must not qualify.
