Ask Crump can produce incorrect, incomplete, outdated, or unexpected output. Verify important information with authoritative sources and qualified professionals.

Conversational responses are generated through configured AI providers. Image requests may be sent to OpenAI. Current-information requests may use Brave Search or OpenWeather. Provider availability and behavior may change.

Credentials and payment data

Passwords are stored as one-way hashes. Session, verification, and reset credentials are stored in hashed form. Ask Crump does not receive complete payment-card numbers from Stripe or the platform stores.

Retention

Account and conversation data remains available while the account is active unless deleted sooner. Security, billing, fraud-prevention, backup, and legally required records may follow separate retention periods.

User controls

Users can report an AI response for safety review without leaving the application, delete conversations, export conversation history, revoke device sessions, disable check-ins and notifications, and permanently delete the account. Safety reports are deleted with the owning account. Deleting the Ask Crump account does not automatically cancel an Apple or Google subscription; that subscription must also be managed through the applicable store.

Security

The service uses encrypted transport, HTTP-only web cookies, secure native credential storage, account-scoped caches, rate limits, and server-side ownership checks. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

Subscriptions and billing

Web subscriptions are processed by Stripe. iOS and Android subscriptions are processed through the applicable platform store and RevenueCat. Prices, taxes, renewal terms, refund rights, and cancellation methods are shown by the billing provider before purchase.

Subscriptions renew until canceled through the provider that processed the purchase. Deleting an account does not itself cancel a store subscription.

Age requirement

Ask Crump is intended for users age 17 or older. Users must also satisfy any higher minimum age required by local law or the applicable platform. The service is not designed for children and should not be used in environments that require child-directed controls unless those controls are separately implemented and verified.
