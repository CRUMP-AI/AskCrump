HOW IT WORKS

Create the boundary, then continue inside it.

Projects do not silently absorb every chat. You name the workspace and choose the instructions, context, conversations, and files that belong there.

Name the Project and its purpose

Create a clear boundary for one initiative, client engagement, research effort, manuscript, campaign, or other body of work.

Add instructions and durable context

Record goals, tone, rules, constraints, canon, timeline details, notes, and decisions that should survive between sessions.

Connect the relevant work

Attach owned conversations and supported files, then keep related manuscript or generated work with the same Project.

Open the Project when you return

YOU CONTROL THE BOUNDARY

Continuity should be deliberate.

Projects help organize context; they do not remove the need to review it.

  • Keep sensitive material out of a Project unless you are authorized to use it with the service.
  • Review Project instructions and canon when requirements, facts, or approvals change.
  • Verify important factual, legal, medical, financial, compliance, and safety-sensitive output independently.
  • Archive or update work that should no longer guide future conversations.
COMMON QUESTIONS

What to expect from Ask Crump Projects.

What can I keep in a Project?

A Project can hold its name, description, instructions, durable notes or canon, connected conversations, supported reference files, and related manuscript work.

Does every new conversation become a Project?

No. A new conversation remains separate unless you choose to use or attach it to a Project.

How many Projects can I create for free?

The current Free plan supports up to 2 active private Projects. Professional supports 25 and Enterprise supports 200. Plan limits may be reviewed in the app before purchase.

Will a Project remember everything automatically?

No. Projects provide a durable boundary for the instructions, notes, files, and conversations you deliberately connect. Review that context and keep it current.

Can I open the same Project on another device?

Yes. Sign in to the same Ask Crump account, open Projects, and select the named Project you want to continue.
