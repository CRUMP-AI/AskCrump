HOW IT WORKS

Direct one shot at a time.

A restrained brief is easier to generate and easier to judge. Describe the essential subject and action first, then add only the camera, environment, light, style, and constraints the shot truly needs.

Write the scene direction

Name the subject, action, setting, composition, camera movement, light, mood, and any details that must remain absent.

Choose Quick, Extendable, or Cinematic

Match the mode to the task. The studio shows the available duration, quality choices, and current credit cost before generation.

Generate and inspect the result

Review identity consistency, anatomy, motion, continuity, text, logos, artifacts, safety, and whether the shot supports the intended message.

Keep, revise, or continue

Completed clips are delivered to your account. Refine the brief for another take or continue a compatible video from its ending when that option is available.

GENERATE WITH INTENT

COMMON QUESTIONS

What to expect from Ask Crump video.

Is Ask Crump video generation free?

The Ask Crump workspace has a free plan, but video generations use Crump Credits. The studio shows the current cost for the selected mode, duration, and quality before generation.

What is the difference between the video modes?

Quick is designed for efficient short first takes, Extendable can continue compatible generated clips from their ending, and Cinematic is the premium path for higher-intent short scenes. Current options are shown in the studio.

Can Ask Crump continue an existing video?

Extendable mode can continue supported generated clips when the source is compatible with the continuation workflow. Not every uploaded or generated video is eligible.

How long does generation take?

Timing varies with the selected mode, provider capacity, duration, and quality. The job runs asynchronously, so the interface can show progress while the provider completes the work.

Where does the finished video go?

Completed video is delivered through your authenticated Ask Crump account and can be kept in your private Library. Always review the final clip before publishing or sharing it.
