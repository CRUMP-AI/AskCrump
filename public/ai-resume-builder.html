WHAT IT IS BUILT FOR

A stronger first draft grounded in facts you can defend.

An AI résumé builder should help you decide what matters, organize it clearly, and make revision easier. It should never manufacture credentials, employers, dates, metrics, or accomplishments.

01 · TARGETING

Tailor a résumé to a role

Compare a job description with your real background, then emphasize the most relevant skills and evidence without copying unsupported language.

02 · REFINEMENT

Strengthen existing material

Turn rough notes or an older résumé into clearer sections, tighter bullets, consistent tense, and a more deliberate hierarchy.

03 · APPLICATION

Draft the supporting story

Use the same verified facts to prepare a cover-letter draft, interview themes, or a concise professional summary for review.

HOW IT WORKS

Start with evidence, not adjectives.

Give Ask Crump the target role and the accurate details behind your work: responsibilities, tools, results, dates, education, credentials, and any constraints the résumé must respect.

Provide your real background

Paste accurate experience or attach a supported source file. Include the target job description when you have permission to use it.

Name the role and deliverable

Ask for an editable Word résumé and specify the role, seniority, location, length, required sections, and any relevant industry conventions.

Challenge every bullet

Check employers, titles, dates, tools, education, credentials, metrics, and accomplishments. Remove anything you cannot support in an interview.

Download, edit, and continue

Open the .docx in your document editor, make the final visual and wording changes, and return to the conversation when another role needs a different emphasis.

ACCURACY OVER EMBELLISHMENT

A polished résumé still has to be true.

Ask Crump can improve structure and language, but you remain responsible for the final application.

  • Never add a credential, employer, title, date, skill, metric, or accomplishment that is not accurate.
  • ATS-friendly formatting does not guarantee parsing, ranking, interviews, or employment.
  • Review spelling, chronology, contact details, links, confidentiality, and employer-specific requirements.
  • Generated output can vary by request; the page does not promise a perfect or job-winning résumé.
COMMON QUESTIONS

What to expect from Ask Crump résumés.

Does Ask Crump create an editable Word résumé?

Yes. When the request completes successfully, Ask Crump can package the résumé as an editable .docx file. Review it in your document editor before applying.

Will Ask Crump invent achievements to make me sound stronger?

It should not. Provide accurate facts and verify the result. If a useful metric is missing, the safer response is to identify the gap and ask you for evidence rather than fabricate a number.

Is the output guaranteed to pass every applicant tracking system?

No. The résumé profile uses restrained, readable conventions, but applicant tracking systems and employer workflows vary. Check the final file against the application’s requirements.

Can I use an existing résumé or job description?

You can paste source material or attach supported files that you have permission to use. Ask Crump can use them as context, subject to product file and plan limits.

Where does the generated file go?

Generated files are delivered through your authenticated Ask Crump account and can be kept in your private Library. A Project can keep related conversations and reference material together.
