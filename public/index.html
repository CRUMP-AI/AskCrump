WHAT ASK CRUMP HAS BECOME

The answer is only the beginning.

A good assistant can answer a question. Ask Crump is being built around what happens after that answer turns into work.

01 · CONTINUITY

Work keeps its context.

Conversations synchronize to your account, Projects isolate durable instructions and reference material, and long-form work can resume instead of restarting.

02 · CREATION

Make something you can keep.

Research, images, documents, manuscripts, and video live beside the conversation rather than disappearing into separate tools and tabs.

03 · OWNERSHIP

Your work has a home.

Generated and uploaded assets stay in a private Saved Library, while Project context remains attached to the work it belongs to.

HOW CLEVER CRUMP BUILDS

Useful first. Durable second. Flashy only when it earns its place.

Ask Crump keeps improving because a paid product should keep earning its place: understandable, trustworthy, durable, and increasingly useful every time you return.

01 · KEEP THE USER ORIENTED

Advanced should still feel understandable.

Tools are grouped by outcomes, expensive features show their boundaries, and the interface should explain what happens next without making users learn provider jargon.

02 · PROTECT THE WORK

Projects and private storage over disposable output.

When something matters, it should have somewhere to live, a way to continue, and a clear path back into the workspace.

03 · PROTECT THE BUSINESS

Premium compute needs real economics.

Subscriptions unlock capability while Crump Credits meter expensive generation. Server-side budget controls remain separate from the customer-facing wallet.

04 · KEEP IMPROVING

The product is allowed to grow up.

We would rather replace awkward seams, fix small details, and strengthen the architecture than pretend an early interface is permanent because it already works.
