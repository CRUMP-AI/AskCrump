WHY ASK CRUMP?
A quick answer is useful. Some work needs somewhere to live.

Ask Crump earns its place when you're coming back tomorrow: a novel with canon, a business you're building, research that keeps changing, a document that needs another revision, or a Project with files and decisions you don't want to explain all over again.

BUILT FOR WORK YOU COME BACK TO

Keep the context.
Keep moving.

The goal isn't to add more buttons. It's to make the things you already do with AI feel connected instead of scattered.

01 · PROJECTS

The work gets its own world.

Keep chats, instructions, reference files, canon, notes, manuscripts, and created assets together. A novel stays separate from a business plan. A client Project stays separate from everything else.

02 · CRUMP

Helpful doesn't have to mean agreeable.

Crump can help build the idea, tell you when something doesn't add up, catch continuity problems, and still talk like a person instead of turning every conversation into a disclaimer.

03 · LONG-FORM

Write past the chat box.

Build manuscripts by section, preserve Project context, revise without losing the larger work, and export real documents—including book interiors with KDP-aware settings.

04 · CREATION

Research, files, images, video, documents.

The tools live beside the Project instead of in separate worlds. What you research and create can stay attached to the work it belongs to.

ONE PLACE TO KEEP GOING

Start messy.
Organize when it matters.

You shouldn't need a perfect setup before you can think. Start a normal conversation. When the idea becomes real work, give it a Project, add the reference material, and keep going from there.

1Talk it through.Ask, brainstorm, challenge an idea, or hand Crump a file.
2Give the work a home.Projects keep the conversations, files, rules, canon, and decisions that matter.
3Make something.Draft the chapter, research the issue, create the image, export the document, or build the next deliverable.
4Come back later.The Project still knows what belongs to it, so you spend less time rebuilding context.
CHOOSE ASK CRUMP WHEN…

The answer isn't the finish line.

There are excellent general-purpose assistants for quick questions. Ask Crump is being shaped around the moments when the conversation turns into ongoing work.

CONTINUITY

You don't want to explain it again.

Keep Project instructions, source material, canon, and prior work attached to the thing you're actually building.

DELIVERABLES

You need more than an answer.

Move toward manuscripts, documents, images, videos, exports, and other things you can actually use outside the conversation.

POINT OF VIEW

You want an assistant with some backbone.

Crump can disagree, question the plan, notice a contradiction, and help you improve it without becoming stiff or combative.

LONG-FORM

The work is bigger than one prompt.

Organize books, chapters, sections, revisions, canon, and supporting references without treating every session like a blank page.

OWNERSHIP

Your files should belong with the Project.

Reference material and things created inside the Project stay visible together instead of disappearing into disconnected chats.

COST CLARITY

Premium tools should have boundaries.

Image and video generation are designed around explicit plan and credit limits rather than pretending expensive provider calls are free forever.
